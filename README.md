# Business Analyst Case Study: Insurance Prior Authorization Workflow
Business Analyst case study eliminating a 5-7 day dental insurance prior-authorization bottleneck through "As-Is" vs. "To-Be" process mapping and digital workflow redesign.

## Executive Overview
This portfolio project demonstrates a Business Analysis case study for an operational workflow redesign in a healthcare setting. The project addresses the 5-7 day dental insurance prior-authorization bottleneck through "As-Is" vs. "To-Be" process mapping and digital workflow. With integrating a digital clearinghouse, the workflow process will improve the dental insurance prior-authorization time from 5-7 days to 24 hours.  

---

## Problem vs. Solution Overview

### Current Manual State ("As-Is")
* **Chairside Entry:** Dentist diagnoses the patient; Dental Assistant enters the treatment plan into the EHR system and prints a hard copy.
* **Manual Paperwork:** Receptionist transcribes data onto a 4-page paper claim form and prints physical diagnostic X-rays.
* **Fax Transmission:** Receptionist faxes the paper packet to the insurance carrier.
* **Insurance Delay (Bottleneck):** Carrier queues the fax, requiring 3–5 business days for manual review before faxing back a decision.
* **Manual Follow-up:** Receptionist checks the physical fax machine, updates the patient record, and initiates phone tag to schedule the appointment.

### Proposed Digital State ("To-Be")
* **Digital Capture:** Dental Assistant enters the treatment plan and attaches digital X-rays directly in the EHR chairside.
* **1-Click API Submission:** System transmits claim data and digital artifacts directly to an integrated insurance clearinghouse portal.
* **Real-Time Verification:** Automated rule checks deliver instant approvals or route complex cases to a fast-track 24-hour digital queue.
* **Automated Status Update:** Front-desk dashboard reflects approval status instantly, enabling the receptionist to confirm co-pays and schedule the patient before they leave the office.

---

## Key Business Outcomes & ROI Targets
* **Turnaround Time:** Reduce prior-authorization cycle time from 5–7 business days to **under 24 hours** (with real-time approvals for standard procedures).
* **Operational Efficiency:** Eliminate **100% of physical faxing** and paper claim forms for prior-authorizations.
* **Schedule Optimization:** Increase same-day treatment scheduling rates by **40%**, reducing patient drop-off and open chair time.
